  <h1>About</h1>

  <p>
    <strong>The Observer Effect</strong> is a personal knowledge journal where I write about
    technology, science, and the ideas that shape our understanding of the world.
  </p>

  <p>
    In quantum mechanics, the observer effect refers to the phenomenon where the act of
    observation fundamentally changes the system being observed. In much the same way,
    I believe that the act of reading, thinking, and writing about ideas transforms both
    the ideas and ourselves.
  </p>

  <p>
    Here, you'll find articles ranging from deep technical explorations to high-level
    scientific concepts, practical tutorials, and the occasional opinion piece. Every
    article is written with care, aiming for clarity and depth.
  </p>
